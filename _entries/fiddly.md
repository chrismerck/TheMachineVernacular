---
headword: fiddly
pos: adj.
class: vogue
senses:
  - label: engineering
    definition: >
      Requiring many small, interdependent steps, each simple alone but
      error-prone in combination; transferred from manual work with small
      parts to setup, configuration, and bring-up procedures — as distinct
      from *hard* (conceptually difficult) or *tedious* (merely long).
    example: >
      Bringing up a peripheral by writing its registers over the debugger
      is fiddly.
first_use:
  date: "1926"
  type: published
  url: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/fiddly
  source: Merriam-Webster, "fiddly"
  note: >
    from *fiddle* (v., 1530, "make aimless or frivolous movements") +
    *-y*; the OED's earliest citation is *Blackwood's Magazine*, 1926,
    "requiring time or dexterity; pernickety". Chiefly British, absent
    from American print until 1984. Machine speech carries it from
    handwork to procedures: register sequences, toolchains, configuration
