---
headword: rider
pos: n.
class: neosemy
senses:
  - label: software engineering
    definition: >
      A change attached to a branch or pull request whose purpose it does
      not share, so as to merge on that work's momentum rather than face
      review on its own; as distinct from scope creep, a rider is complete
      and separable — it is only traveling.
    example: >
      Flipping the allocator default deserves its own design pass, not a
      rider on this branch.
etymology: >
  From the legislative sense ("a clause attached to a bill to pass with
  it, often unrelated to its subject"), transferred to version control.
