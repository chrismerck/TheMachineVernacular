---
headword: governor
pos: n.
class: vogue
senses:
  - label: software engineering
    definition: >
      A configured ceiling treated as the fixed point of a design: other
      sizes and limits are derived to fit beneath it, and a proposal that
      would exceed it must reshape itself rather than move it.
    example: >
      The message-size cap is the governor — chunk size derives from it,
      and no revision may touch it.
