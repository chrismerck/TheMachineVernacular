---
headword: muscle memory
pos: n., often attrib.
class: neosemy
senses:
  - label: AI agents
    definition: >
      An output habit entrenched by training-data frequency that fires
      despite explicit contrary instruction present in context; as distinct
      from forgetting — the rule is known, even recited, and still outrun by
      the habitual completion. Suppressed reliably by mechanical guards, not
      by reminders.
    example: >
      It re-read its own warning about that flag this morning and still
      typed it mid-diagnosis — that's muscle memory; the fix is a deny hook,
      not a fourth reminder.
etymology: >
  Figurative extension of *muscle memory* ("procedural motor learning"),
  transferred from human motor skill to the statistical entrenchment of
  token patterns in a trained model.
